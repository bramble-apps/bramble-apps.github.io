Get Bramble on Obtainium

Obtainium installs Android apps straight from their GitHub releases and keeps them up to date. Pick an app, or add all four at once.

Open in Obtainium Get Obtainium first

How it works

  1. Install Obtainium if you don't have it yet, from its GitHub releases (the app-arm64-v8a-release.apk file) or from F-Droid.
  2. On your phone or tablet, tap Open in Obtainium. Obtainium asks whether to import the app: confirm it.
  3. Open the app in Obtainium and tap Install. From then on, Obtainium tells you when a new version is out.
